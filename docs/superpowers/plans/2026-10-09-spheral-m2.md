## Review focus

1. **Stress invariance under mass scaling.** Scaling multiplies masses, densities and the free density by one factor
   s and leaves every stress, pressure law and modulus unchanged; the deceleration follows from the scaled masses.
   Anything that divides a force by a mass (loads, deceleration, damping) uses the scaled mass consistently. → Tasks 3,
   4, 5, 6.
2. **One curve.** The particle's energy is the finite element's enthalpy through the M1 table; T comes back through the
   table's exact inverse; Spheral's own temperature relations are never used (spec §8.1, §2 traps). → Tasks 3, 4.
3. **The EOS's sound speed and bulk modulus agree with its own dP/dρ** (spec §2: the Grüneisen trap). → Task 3.
4. **Nothing is tuned to a reference.** Checks 4–6 compare with exact or finite-element solutions; the drag of the
   loads on particles is compared with the frames' own integrated loads (M1 decision 15), the history's drag a
   diagnostic. → Tasks 6, 8.
5. **Every removal is booked** with its mass and enthalpy into the film account; the accounts close to round-off
   through `analyse`. → Task 5.
6. **Every labelled device is a setting with a recorded default and bracket**: crossings per interval, the damping
   ratio, the strain-rate floor, the surface-detection threshold, the area-share calibration. → Tasks 3–6.

## Plan decisions (Asha to confirm; see Questions)

- **D1, reference positions** (Question 2). The finite-element body is a fixed Lagrangian frame that only recedes, so
  each particle keeps its **reference position** (where it was built). Its temperature is sampled there, and it is
  removed when the next frame's body no longer contains its reference position. Its current position serves the
  mechanics only. Then sampling depends on the frame alone, so the body stage precomputes each particle's temperature
  at every frame and the frame at which it leaves (Task 2), and the runner only interpolates in time.
- **D2, the energy in the replay is imposed and unscaled.** u = h(T) − h(300 K) from the M1 table, overwritten every
  step from the imposed temperature, never a scaled quantity. The switch to a window (spec §9.3) therefore divides
  masses, densities and free densities by s and leaves u unchanged, instead of "energies multiplied by it".
- **D3, own checkpoints.** At every frame boundary the runner writes a checkpoint of its own (`checkpoint_<k>.npz`:
  IDs, reference and current positions, velocities, H, masses, densities, u, deviatoric stress, plastic strain and
  rate, the scale factor), gathered to rank 0 in ID order, and resumes or branches from it. Spheral's restart files
  are not used: a window has to convert the state anyway (D2), and a checkpoint in ID order is independent of the
  domain decomposition.
- **D4, the bodies are lattices clipped by the finite-element body.** The spec's polyhedron (§6.2) would need a closed
  manifold surface, which the frames are not after element deaths (up to 288 non-manifold edges, M1). A lattice point
  belongs to the body when the frame's `TetLocator` contains it, the same test as removal. The centroidally relaxed
  generator stays an option for later; M2 uses the lattice.
- **D5, damping** is mass-proportional, a = −2ζω₁v with ω₁ = π c_l,s / D (the lowest breathing mode's order, at the
  scaled sound speed), default ζ = 0.05, bracket 0, 0.05, 0.2; check 4 confirms that the balanced state does not move.
- **D6, the strength's strain rate** is the plastic strain rate Spheral hands `yieldStrength`, floored at
  ε̇_min = 10⁻⁶ 1/s (a labelled device, bracket 10⁻⁷ and 10⁻⁵): Chen's law gives zero flow stress at zero rate, so
  without a floor an elastic particle would yield at zero stress. Question 4.

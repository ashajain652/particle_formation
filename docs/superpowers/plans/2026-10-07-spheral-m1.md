## Facts established while writing this plan (2026-10-07)

Read from the code on `main`, the Step 3 plan's code blocks (`2026-09-20-melt-spraying.md`, Task 10) and the
sub-plans' amendments (`melt-spraying-subplans/`). Items marked **(confirm)** are confirmed or corrected on the
reconstructed prototype's real frames by Task 10, Step 2; Task 11 writes the outcome into the spec.

1. **What `main` exports today** (`reentry_model/coupled.py::write_vtk_frame`): `field_<k>.vtu` with nodal `T` on the
   active tetrahedra; `surface_<k>.vtp` with nodal `T` and per-patch `q_conv`, `q_rad`, `T_patch`. Both files carry
   **all** mesh nodes (`mesh.points`, dead ones included, unreferenced); the vtu's cells are the active tetrahedra only.
   Frames are written every `frames_every` macro steps (frame `k` at macro step `k · frames_every`, the first before any
   step), indexed in `field.pvd` / `surface.pvd` with the time printed `{:.6g}`.
2. **What Step 3 adds** (plan Task 10 and sub-plan 10's amendments of 2026-09-27 and 2026-10-02): in the vtu, nodal
   `liquid_fraction` (= `material.liquid_fraction(T)`) and per-cell `phi` (the element's remaining fraction); in the
   vtp, per patch `film_thickness` [m], `film_T` [K], `we_s`, `closure` (0 Girin, 1 Couette), `kn_local`, `p_w` [Pa],
   `tau` [Pa], `r_droplet` [m, NaN where nothing released], `release_rate` = `res.dm / areas` — **kg/m² per macro step,
   not per second** — `delta_m` [m, NaN where the closure is not Girin's, on faces the step's deaths exposed and before
   the first evaluation] and `deep_thickness` [m, m_d/(ρ_l A); a mass per area]. The flow and spray fields are carried
   across a step's element deaths by face id; on faces the deaths exposed `p_w` and `tau` default to **0.0, not NaN**.
3. **`p_w` and `tau` are zero in every frame whose step had no film** (confirm). `MeltingBody._film_and_spray` sets
   `last_flow = None` when `m_f.sum() <= 0`, and the writer then writes the 0.0 default. The surface flow itself is
   evaluated every step (`delta_m` is written from every evaluation), so the values exist but are not exported. On the
   100 mm flight this means no loads before melt onset (about 25.5 s), while the replay starts at the beginning of the
   flight (spec §6.4). Question 3.
4. **The history** (`<outdir>/<run>.csv`, 9 significant digits, one row per macro step) has `time_s`, `altitude_km`,
   `velocity_kms`, `mass_kg`, `flight_path_deg`, `lat_deg`, `lon_deg`, `density_kgm3`, `dynamic_pressure_Pa`,
   `load_factor_g` (= |a_drag| / g₀) and, with melting, `p_w_stag_Pa`, `film_mass_kg`, `deep_mass_kg`,
   `sprayed_mass_kg` and the other `MELT_COLUMNS`. **There is no deceleration column** (it is `load_factor_g · g₀`),
   **no air temperature** (needed by the record's flight state, spec §11.2) and no drag force (it is
   `mass_kg · load_factor_g · g₀`).
5. **Run directory layout:** `<outdir>/<run>.csv`, `<outdir>/<run>.json`, `<outdir>/<run>/vtk/{field,surface}_<k>.{vtu,vtp}`
   and `{field,surface}.pvd`. The run JSON's `settings` hold the atmosphere, wind, `macro_step_s`, `frames_every` and
   (Step 3, amendment of 2026-10-05) `seed`; the material's key is set by Step 3's CLI (confirm its name). The body
   frame's flight direction `v_hat` is not written anywhere; it is `(1, 0, 0)` by default in every body class (confirm).
6. **Mass of the finite-element body** = Σ φ_e ρ V_e over active elements + film + deep account
   (`MeltingBody.mass`). The active tetrahedra's union is therefore not the body's volume once elements are partly
   consumed (φ < 1).
7. **The surface** is the set of faces used by exactly one active element, oriented outward (`mesh.boundary_faces`,
   `SurfaceMesh.normals`). It is closed as an oriented surface by construction, but after element death it can be
   non-manifold (an edge or a vertex shared by two tetrahedra that touch only there). Whether Spheral's polyhedron
   builder (M2) accepts that is open; Task 4 counts such edges and vertices.
8. **The finite-element material.** `reentry_model.material.Material` on `main` is Step 2's (no latent heat). Step 3's
   (sub-plan 02, amendments of 2026-09-27 and 2026-09-28) evaluates h(T) exactly as a piecewise quadratic on its own
   nodes (`_T_h`, `_h_nodes`, `_latent_slope`; the c_p nodes, 293 K, the melting range and the Scheil curve's 1 K
   nodes), with the zero of enthalpy at **T_REF = 293 K**; f_l is `np.interp` on `(_fl_T, _fl)` (Scheil tabulated at
   1 K, the 3.6 % eutectic remainder over ±2 K at 750 K); `T_feed` = 910 K; liquid 2,400 kg/m³, 1.3 mPa·s, 0.80 N/m.
   **`AA7075_scheil` exists only in the prototype until Step 3 lands**, so `prepare` must import the `reentry_model`
   that produced the run (Global constraints).
9. **The Step 3 prototype changed after the spec's last look at it:** the molten cascade (amendment of 2026-10-03,
   `--molten-cascade on|off`, default on, the backlog fix Asha chose) and the seed (2026-10-05, `--seed`, default
   12345, run names ending `_seed-<n>` otherwise). The measurements of facts 46–68 used **`AA7075_range`**, not
   `AA7075_scheil` (Question 7).
10. **Two Pythons, two numpys.** `drama_env` has numpy 2.5.3, scipy 1.18.1 and pyvista 0.49.0 (rebuilt 2026-10-06,
    without pyDRAMA); Spheral's Python in the image has numpy 1.26.4 (`pin.json`). Files passed between them are

11. **Documentation targets.** The README has no Spheral section; `docs/model_assumptions.md` is titled "Steps 1–2"
    and Step 3's Task 15 will add its own numbered sections. Question 8.

mechanical columns). The zero of energy is moved from the finite element's 293 K to the spec's 300 K by subtracting
h_FE(300 K), which is exact in differences.

    and -12.9 % at 50, 100 and 150 s (D_patch: -26 %, -35 %, -37 %). Task 10's drag comparison and the `drag_vs_history`
